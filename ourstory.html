FEASTABLES BEGAN AS A FUN, BETTER-FOR-YOU SNACK BRAND

Inspired by MrBeast's journey with Crohn's and his mission to make great-tasting, accessible snacks. What started as a dream to prove that better ingredients could mean bolder flavors quickly became a movement.

OUR FIRST LAUNCH — THE MRBEAST BAR WASN'T JUST A CHOCOLATE BAR

It was a flavor-packed adventure, complete with gamified experiences, fan engagement, and over-the-top stunts. Every bar was a ticket to something bigger — golden tickets, cash prizes, and memories that brought millions together.

AS WE GREW, SO DID OUR STANDARDS
& OUR RESPONSIBILITY

For what we make and what we stand for. We reformulated our recipes for an even bigger, bolder flavor and redesigned our packaging and bars to match. Every ingredient was reconsidered, every supplier re-evaluated.

ALONG THE WAY WE UNCOVERED

THE HARD TRUTH BEHIND THE COCOA INDUSTRY

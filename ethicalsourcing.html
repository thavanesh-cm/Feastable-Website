FAQ?

Fairtrade certification ensures that farmers and workers are paid a fair, minimum price for their goods, protecting them from volatile market drops. It also provides an additional Premium to invest in their communities.

The Living Income Reference Price is the price a farmer needs to receive for their crop to earn a living income. We actively work to match this price, closing the gap between poverty and a decent standard of living.

Child Labor Monitoring and Remediation Systems (CLMRS) are processes set up within supply chains to identify cases of child labor, provide remediation to affected children and families, and prevent future occurrences.
